StudyFetch is an AI study platform that records lectures, generates notes, builds a study plan around your deadlines, and creates flashcards, quizzes, and practice tests from your uploads, with an AI tutor named Sparky. BrainLift is an iOS and web app that turns photos of notes, PDFs, typed notes, or a topic into quizzes and flashcard decks, then turns wrong answers into a review deck. Both use AI on your own material, but StudyFetch aims wider and BrainLift aims narrower.

## The short version

StudyFetch clearly wins on breadth. Live Lecture records your class while Sparky writes notes, a study plan organizes your sessions around your deadlines, and Arcade turns course material into game-style challenges. It accepts many kinds of uploads, including slides, documents, videos, lecture recordings, and YouTube links, and it runs on web, iPhone, and Android. If you want one app that follows you from the lecture hall to exam day, StudyFetch covers more of that path.

BrainLift does fewer things and keeps them quick. Snap a page or upload a PDF, choose difficulty and question count, and you are practicing. Wrong answers become a "practice my mistakes" deck with explanations that feeds into spaced review, and XP, daily quests, and streaks give you a reason to return. Its Premium plan is also priced lower than StudyFetch's Premium plans listed on the App Store.

## Where StudyFetch is the better choice

If you want to capture a lecture as it happens, StudyFetch has a feature built for exactly that. Live Lecture records class while the AI writes notes, so you leave with material ready to study. BrainLift does not record audio, so you would need to bring your own notes or slides.

StudyFetch's study plan is the other standout. It builds a schedule around your materials and deadlines, which helps if your main problem is knowing what to study and when. Arcade adds game-style practice, and Tutor Me offers help around the clock.

It also supports Android, which BrainLift does not, and it handles a wider range of uploads, including video and YouTube links. If you want a single app to organize your whole course, StudyFetch is the more complete option.

## Where BrainLift is the better choice

BrainLift keeps the steps between your notes and your first question small. One photo or PDF becomes a quiz and a deck, at the difficulty and length you pick. If finals are close and you need practice now, our [finals week study plan](/blog/finals-week-study-plan/) shows how to fit quick quizzes into a tight schedule.

The mistakes loop is where BrainLift puts its focus. Every question you miss can become a card with an explanation in a "practice my mistakes" deck, and those cards come back on a spaced schedule until they stick. Our guide to [learning from practice test mistakes](/blog/learn-from-practice-test-mistakes/) explains why reviewing errors pays off.

You also get XP, levels, daily quests, streaks, and achievements, plus an AI tutor chat that can explain topics with charts and diagrams. On price, BrainLift Premium is $6.99 per month or $49.99 per year. AI-generated content can contain mistakes in any app, so check questions against your source.

## Pricing

BrainLift is free to start with usage limits. Premium is $6.99 per month, $49.99 per year, or $2.99 per week, as listed on the App Store.

StudyFetch says getting started is free and offers premium tiers. Its App Store listing shows Premium in-app purchases of $19.99 monthly, $29.00 quarterly, and $96.00 yearly, plus other add-ons. Web prices may differ, so check [StudyFetch's website](https://www.studyfetch.com/) before you subscribe.

## Switching or using both

The two can work side by side. You could record lectures and keep your semester plan in StudyFetch, then use BrainLift to quiz yourself on a chapter and turn your mistakes into review cards.

To move material into BrainLift, export or copy your notes, then upload the PDF or paste the text as a typed note. Students balancing several courses may like our page for [college students](/for/college-students/).

If you want a faster way to practice from the notes you already have, [create a free BrainLift account](/auth/register) and try a quiz on tonight's reading.

## FAQ

### Can BrainLift record lectures like StudyFetch?

No. BrainLift does not record audio. It works from photos of notes, PDFs, typed notes, or a topic you enter, while StudyFetch offers Live Lecture recording.

### Does StudyFetch have an Android app?

StudyFetch says it is available on web, iOS, and Android. BrainLift is available on iPhone, iPad, and the web, but not on Android.

### Which app is cheaper?

Based on App Store listings, BrainLift Premium is $6.99 per month or $49.99 per year, and StudyFetch Premium is $19.99 per month or $96.00 per year. Both have free ways to start.
