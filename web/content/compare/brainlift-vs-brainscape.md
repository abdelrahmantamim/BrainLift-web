Brainscape is a flashcard platform built on confidence-based repetition, where you rate how well you know each card from 1 to 5, with a catalog of expert-certified decks for many exams. BrainLift is an iOS and web app that turns your notes, photos, PDFs, or a topic into quizzes and flashcard decks, then turns wrong answers into a review deck. Both rely on self-rating to schedule review, but they differ in where content comes from and how you are tested.

## The short version

Brainscape clearly wins if you want content that someone else has already checked. Its certified decks cover a range of exams, including nursing and bar prep, and its free Basic plan lets you make unlimited typed cards, import, share, and study a set of preloaded cards with spaced repetition and reminders. It runs on iPhone, Android, and the web, and it has group options for classes and teams.

BrainLift is the better match when your exam is built around your own course material. It generates multiple-choice quizzes, not only flashcards, from a photo or a PDF, with the difficulty and question count you choose. When you miss a question, "practice my mistakes" makes a deck with explanations. An AI tutor chat, XP, daily quests, and streaks round it out.

## Where Brainscape is the better choice

If you are preparing for a standardized exam that Brainscape covers, its certified decks can save you many hours. Instead of making cards and wondering if you missed something, you start with a deck built for that exam. Nursing students working toward the NCLEX are a good example, and our [NCLEX flashcards study guide](/blog/nclex-flashcards-study-guide/) covers how to combine ready-made decks with your own notes.

Brainscape also has a mature, well-defined algorithm. Rating each card from 1 to 5 gives you more detail than a simple right or wrong, and the app uses that to decide how soon you see the card again. If you prefer drilling flashcards to taking quizzes, this approach fits well.

It also works on Android, and its Enterprise option offers group content controls and student study data for teachers and organizations. BrainLift does not have an Android app or team management features.

## Where BrainLift is the better choice

Your professor's exam is usually built from your professor's slides, not a national deck. BrainLift turns those slides, PDFs, and handwritten notes into practice that matches your course. Our guide to turning a [textbook or PDF into practice questions](/blog/textbook-pdf-to-practice-questions/) shows how to set it up.

Quizzes are the other key difference. Multiple-choice questions make you choose between close options, which is how many exams actually test you. BrainLift creates a quiz and a flashcard deck from the same material, and "practice my mistakes" sends each missed question into a deck with an explanation and spaced review.

If you get stuck, the AI tutor chat can explain a topic and draw charts or diagrams. XP, levels, daily quests, streaks, and achievements help you keep a routine. AI-generated content can contain mistakes, so check questions against your source.

## Pricing

BrainLift is free to start with usage limits. Premium costs $6.99 per month, $49.99 per year, or $2.99 per week on the App Store.

Brainscape offers a free Basic plan, a Pro plan with unlimited AI flashcard creation and certified content, and custom pricing for groups. The billing term for Pro was not clear to us, so check the [official Brainscape pricing page](https://www.brainscape.com/pricing) for current rates.

## Switching or using both

Using both can make sense. You might study a certified Brainscape deck for exam-wide coverage, then use BrainLift to quiz yourself on the parts your class emphasizes and to catch mistakes from practice sessions.

If you want to bring Brainscape cards into BrainLift, paste the content into a typed note and generate a quiz or deck from it. Nursing students can also visit our page for [nursing students](/for/nursing-students/).

When you are ready to test it on your own lecture slides, [download BrainLift on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brainlift-ai-quiz-flashcard/id6749842070) and create your first quiz.

## FAQ

### Does BrainLift have certified decks like Brainscape?

No. BrainLift has no pre-made library. You build quizzes and decks from your own material or a topic, and you can share them by link or QR code.

### How is confidence-based repetition different from BrainLift's review?

Both ask you to rate yourself. Brainscape uses a 1 to 5 confidence scale, while BrainLift uses SM-2 style self-rating to schedule each card.

### Can Brainscape make cards from my files?

Yes. Brainscape says its AI can create flashcards from documents, slides, PDFs, spreadsheets, YouTube videos, and photos, with unlimited AI cards on Pro.
