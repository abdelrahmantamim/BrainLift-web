AP exams cover a full year of material in a few hours, mostly through multiple-choice questions and written responses that ask you to apply ideas, not just name them. The 2027 exams run May 3–7 and May 10–14, so your September notes have to survive until spring.

## Where studying for AP exams breaks down

### Fall units fade by spring

You learned cellular respiration in October, and by April it feels like someone else's notes. That is normal forgetting, and it is fixable with regular review. Photograph your notes after each unit, let BrainLift turn them into a quiz, and keep the cards in spaced review all year.

### Review books do not match your class

A review book covers the course framework, but your teacher picked the examples, labs, and emphasis. Quizzes made from your own class notes test the version of the course you actually took, and you can add a review book chapter as a PDF to fill gaps.

### Motivation drops in the middle of the year

Studying for a test that is months away is hard to start. BrainLift gives you XP, levels, streaks, and daily quests, so ten minutes after school counts toward something. Share a quiz with your study group by link or QR code and you can quiz each other before a unit test.

## What a generated quiz looks like for AP

**1. (AP Biology) Where do the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis occur?**
A) Stroma
B) Thylakoid membrane (correct)
C) Mitochondrial matrix
D) Cytoplasm
*Why:* The photosystems and electron transport chain sit in the thylakoid membranes inside the chloroplast.

**2. (AP Biology) Which products of the light-dependent reactions power the Calvin cycle?**
A) Glucose and oxygen
B) Carbon dioxide and water
C) ATP and NADPH (correct)
D) Pyruvate and NADH
*Why:* ATP supplies energy and NADPH supplies electrons to fix carbon in the stroma.

**3. (AP U.S. History) The Louisiana Purchase was completed during which presidency?**
A) George Washington
B) John Adams
C) Thomas Jefferson (correct)
D) James Madison
*Why:* Jefferson's administration bought the territory from France in 1803.

## Study habits that work for AP exams

Start reviewing in the fall, not in April. Short sessions spread across months beat one long cram, because each time you pull something from memory it gets easier to recall next time. A major review of study techniques rated practice testing and spaced practice as high utility and rated rereading and highlighting as low ([Dunlosky et al., 2013](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/which-study-strategies-make-the-grade.html)).

Mix subjects within a week, and save full practice exams for the last month.
